# Portfolio

Chinnapat Limprathan's public portfolio and blog: what hiring managers read to decide whether to talk to him.

## Language

### Identity

**Profile**:
The single record of who Chinnapat is: name, headline, summary, availability, and contact links.
_Avoid_: About, bio, user

**Resume**:
The one public PDF of Chinnapat's work history, served from the site and linked wherever a visitor might want it.
_Avoid_: CV, résumé page

**Experience**:
One dated role at one employer, with its highlights.
_Avoid_: Job, position, work history entry

### Work

**Case Study**:
An in-depth write-up of one piece of shipped work: the problem, Chinnapat's role, the key decisions, and the results.
_Avoid_: Project page, portfolio item, work item

**Selected Work**:
The short, curated set of Case Studies featured on the home page.
_Avoid_: Featured projects, highlights

### Writing

**Post**:
One blog article written by Chinnapat.
_Avoid_: Article, blog, note

**Draft**:
A Post that exists in the repo but is not published on the site.
_Avoid_: Unpublished post, hidden post
